# Trigger Prompts (for pressure-testing the skill description)

## Should activate

1. "Here are my messy notes from the client call this morning — can you clean these up and pull out who owns what? [notes pasted]"
2. "Turn this into proper minutes of meeting: [transcript pasted]"
3. "I typed these bullet points during standup, can you organize them and tell me what's still unresolved? [notes pasted]"
4. "Summarize this interview and flag any follow-up questions we still need to ask the candidate. [interview notes pasted]"
5. "Can you extract the action items and deadlines from this call transcript? It's pretty unstructured. [transcript pasted]"

## Should NOT activate

1. "Write an invite for our sprint planning meeting next Tuesday at 10am with the design and eng teams."
2. "Can you draft an agenda for tomorrow's budget review meeting? We need to cover Q3 spend and the new hire requests."
3. "What are some good techniques for keeping a meeting on time when people keep going off-topic?"
4. "Write a follow-up email to a client thanking them for their time yesterday, unrelated to any notes I have."
5. "Summarize this research paper on transformer architectures for me — just the key findings."
