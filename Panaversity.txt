Langchain
======================================================================================================================================================
- LangChain is a framework for building applications that use large language models (LLMs) like OpenAI’s GPT, Claude, or others. 
- It’s designed to make it easier to combine LLMs with external data, tools, and workflows.

LangChain helps developers:
- Connect LLMs to databases, APIs, or documents.
- Build intelligent agents that can reason, plan, and act.
- Manage conversation state, memory, and context over multiple interactions.
- Automate tasks like summarization, question-answering, and multi-step reasoning.
