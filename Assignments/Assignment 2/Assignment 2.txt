Exercise 2:  Knows vs. Guesses
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

Answer:
- The AI was accurate on general knowledge questions but could not confidently answer current or location-specific questions. 
- This showed that AI knows stable facts better than recent or changing information.


Bottom Line
* A confident answer is not necessarily a correct answer. Always ask: "How would the AI know this?"
* Always ask 'How would the AI even know this?' For recent, local, or private facts, make it search or admit uncertainty."



Exercise 3: The 3 Retrieval Modes

Q: Which prompts made the AI actually search? How could you tell?

| Retrieval Mode | Did AI Search? | How Could I Tell?                                   |

| Pretrained     | No             | No citations; answered from existing knowledge.     |
| Web Search     | Yes            | Used current information and cited sources.         |
| Deep Research  | Yes            | Provided detailed analysis with multiple citations. |


| Observation        | Explanation                                                               |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pretrained mode    | Used for stable facts that do not change frequently.                      |
| Web Search mode    | Used for current or time-sensitive information.                           |
| Deep Research mode | Used for comprehensive analysis requiring multiple sources and citations. |


- I could tell because the AI provided citations and current sources, while the Romeo and Juliet summary came from existing knowledge.
- Your wording determines whether AI answers from memory, searches the web, or performs deep research.
