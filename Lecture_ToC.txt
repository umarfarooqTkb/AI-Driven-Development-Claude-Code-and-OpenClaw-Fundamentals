  * System instructions (you can configure LLM as per your preferences)
  * e.g: you will always respond in a funny way.
  * you can define restrictions in system instructions
  * Same settings can also be done in ChatGPT

Temperature:
- Creativity level in responses
- it controls the creativity of LLM (by increasing temperature)
- for English writing, you can increase temperature, becoz you need creativity.(e.g. story writing, brainstorming etc)
- for Math, we can set temperature at 0. (we want to follow step by step), merey prompt sy agay peachy na ho
- if we want our response to adhere to our prompt, then we can reduce the temperature, khud sy koi cheze na lay.
- if we want, that model can bring his ideas, then we can increase temperature.
- it sets the internal behavior of LLM
- Thinking Hard feature is not similar to Temperature.
